Returns every fiat receiving account (virtual account) your business holds, newest first. Give a payer the `depositInstructions` of an `active` account, and their bank transfer arrives as a [deposit](/api-reference/collections/deposits/list).

Receiving accounts are opened from the Teel dashboard, not the API. See [Fiat collections](/guides/fiat-collections#before-you-start) for who can open which accounts.

<Info>**Scope** `collections:read` · rate limit `collections_read` (120/min, burst 40)</Info>

## Response

An array of receiving accounts. Each item has these fields:

<ResponseField name="id" type="string" required>Receiving account UUID. Filter deposits by it with `virtualAccountId`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="currency" type="string" required>Currency the account receives, ISO 4217.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status" type="string" required>`pending` while the account is being opened, then `active`, or `rejected` if it could not be opened.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="rails" type="string[]" required>Payment rails the account accepts, for example `ach`, `wire` or `sepa`. Can be empty while `pending`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="settlementMode" type="string" required>
  What happens to money that arrives:
  - `hold`: it stays as fiat in the account and shows in `fiatBalances` on [Get balances](/api-reference/collections/balances).
  - `auto_onramp`: it is converted to `asset` on `chain` and added to your `held` balance.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="asset" type="string">`auto_onramp` accounts only. The stablecoin arriving money is converted into, for example `USDC`. Absent on `hold` accounts.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="chain" type="string">`auto_onramp` accounts only. The chain `asset` is delivered on, for example `polygon` or `ethereum`. Absent on `hold` accounts.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="depositInstructions" type="object">
  What a payer sends to. Present on `active` accounts only. Each field appears only when the account has one.
  <Expandable title="depositInstructions">
    <ResponseField name="accountName" type="string">Account holder or beneficiary name.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="accountNumber" type="string">Account number.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="routingCode" type="string">ABA routing number, sort code or equivalent.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="iban" type="string">IBAN, for accounts that have one (for example EUR and GBP).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="bic" type="string">BIC / SWIFT code.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="bankName" type="string">Receiving bank name.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="bankAddress" type="string">Receiving bank address.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="reference" type="string">When present, the payer **must** quote this on the payment, or it may not be matched to your account.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string" required>ISO 8601.</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `400` | `ENTITY_REQUIRED` | The key's owner belongs to more than one business and `X-Teel-Entity-Id` is missing. |
| `401` | `UNAUTHORIZED` | Missing or invalid key. |
| `403` | `INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE` | Key lacks `collections:read`. |
| `429` | `RATE_LIMITED` | `collections_read` budget exhausted. Wait for `Retry-After`. |
