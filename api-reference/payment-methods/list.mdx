Returns your business's funding sources: the receiving accounts and collections wallets a payout can be paid from. Pass a row's `id` as `userPaymentMethodId` when you execute a payout.

Bank accounts and wallets added by hand in the dashboard are not listed.

<Info>**Scope** `payment_methods:read` · rate limit `default` (60/min, burst 20)</Info>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `400` | `ENTITY_REQUIRED` | The key's owner belongs to more than one business and `X-Teel-Entity-Id` is missing. |
| `400` | `INVALID_PARAMETER` | `X-Teel-Entity-Id` is not a UUID. |
| `401` | `UNAUTHORIZED` | Missing or invalid key. |
| `403` | `FORBIDDEN` | The key's owner is not a member of the business `X-Teel-Entity-Id` names. |
| `403` | `INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE` | Key lacks `payment_methods:read`. |
| `429` | `RATE_LIMITED` | `default` budget exhausted. Wait for `Retry-After`. |
